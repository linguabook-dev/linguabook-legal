Delete Your Account

LinguaBook · Last updated: August 2026

You can delete your LinguaBook account and everything stored with it at any time. There are two ways to do it: from within the app, or by writing to us if you no longer have the app installed.

1. From the app

  1. Open LinguaBook.
  2. Go to Settings → Backup and sync.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. The deletion takes effect immediately.

If you would rather keep your account and only remove what is stored on our servers, use Delete backup instead. It clears your backup and leaves the account in place.

2. Without the app

If LinguaBook is no longer installed, email miguel.dasilvagalvao1994@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with “Account deletion request” as the subject.

We need the request to come from that address so we can confirm the account is yours. Requests are handled within 30 days, and in practice much sooner.

3. What is deleted

  • your account and the email address attached to it;
  • the EPUB files you backed up;
  • any custom fonts you imported;
  • your app settings, your library catalogue, and your reading positions.

This is permanent. None of it can be restored afterwards, by you or by us.

4. What is not deleted

  • The books and settings on your device. They are yours and they stay where they are. To remove them, uninstall the app.
  • Your subscription. It continues, and is cancelled from the Play Store under Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Purchase records. We keep these for as long as accounting obligations require, with no link to the account you deleted.
